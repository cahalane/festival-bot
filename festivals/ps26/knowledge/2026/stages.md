## Disney stage (`disney-stage`) — added 2026-05-29, seaside attractions strip
Disney+ brand-activation stage (Disney+ is a festival partner); ~20:00 slots. MIXED, not DJ-only —
Paula Tape is a DJ but MARTIN is a singer-songwriter, so think "small showcase stage", not a club stage.
- **Paula Tape** ✅ (Thu 20:00) — Chilean DJ (Santiago→Milan), ex-drummer turned selector. Balearic/
  percussive, synth-laden chugging house, polyrhythms. Labels: Rhythm Section, Permanent Vacation; RA-listed.
- **MARTIN** ✅ (Fri 20:00) — = **Martin Urrutia** (confirmed via cross-referencing his IG handle @martinurrrutia).
  Spanish singer/composer/actor, Getxo/Bilbao (b. 2005), Operación Triunfo 12 finalist (like Natalia
  Lacunza). Debut LP *La insolación* (Apr 2026) produced entirely by Hidrogenesse — indie/experimental-
  emotional, Latin-pop edges, intimate & autobiographical. Also acts in the musical series *Mariliendre*.
- **Absolutely** ✅ (Sat 20:00) — British singer-songwriter (she/her) on **Epic Records** (Sony, major).
  2nd album *Paracosm* (20 Feb 2026, via the absolutely.lnk.to/Paracosm smart-link): blends pop,
  electronica, jazz & R&B into cinematic, personal storytelling ("childlike wonder" world-building).
  Singles incl. "No Audience", "Goodbye Glitter", "Natural Disaster". Press: Rolling Stone, Ones to Watch.
