## Crew picks vs Ciutat — how to read it

⚠️ A Clashfinder star sits on the **Fòrum** timetable, so a name appearing in the programme above
is **NOT** confirmed Ciutat attendance. For an act that plays BOTH (Blood Orange, Geese, JVB,
Hannah Diamond, Melt-Banana, femtanyl, Six Sex, Nick León, Gelli Haha, Spill Tab, Hadren, Teo
Planell, Cara Delevingne, paco te quiero, Corte!, Mechatok…), the star is the **FÒRUM set** — the
Ciutat line is just "also playing in the city," an option, never a plan. Only acts ABSENT from the
Fòrum lineup (billy woods, Mogwai, Model/Actriz…) imply the Ciutat show is the intended one.
**Never tell a crew member they're attending a Ciutat show on a name match — confirm first.**

**To get each member's actual Fòrum-vs-Ciutat split:** the `cf_venue_split.py` tool was retired in
the TS migration and its replacement is deferred (not needed until PS27) — until then, classify each
starred highlight by its real venue manually. (Earlier hand-tallied per-member counts here were stale
name-overlap, not attendance, so they've been removed; re-derive from the venue-split when needed.)
Example resolved this way: one crew member's only Ciutat picks are **Sun 7 Jun @ Sala Apolo**
(Ela Minus 20:40, Bassvictim 22:05, JVB 23:30) + femtanyl Mon 8 Jun 00:50 — their Blood
Orange/Geese stars are FÒRUM.
