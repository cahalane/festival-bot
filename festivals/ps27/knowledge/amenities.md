# Site geography & amenities — Parc del Fòrum, PS26 (from the official map + ground-truth reports)

Source: the festival's official Fòrum '26 map (its own artwork — not redistributed here) +
crew ground-truth reports. Legend:
💧 water points (blue droplet) · bar/spritz/cocktail/wine icons = bars · black **WC** = toilets ·
AT = AccessTicket payment · ➕ medical · ℹ info/lost&found · lockers (consigna) · charging · bike parking.
Icon placements are read off the map (small/overlapping) → approximate. Exact food-vendor
positions TBD closer to the festival.

## Cluster nicknames
Crews often coin their own shorthand for a far-flung stage cluster — this deployment had one
for the main/Plenitude cluster (Estrella Damm + Revolut + Plenitude, NE seaside), named for the
long walk to/from it. That kind of local vocabulary belongs in a deployment's own crew file,
not baked into shared festival data.

## Water points 💧 (where to refill)
- **Mains cluster:** YES — at the **seaside (NW) entrance to the cluster**, by the bike
  parking + a WC (far left of the cluster as drawn). *(Corrected 2026-05-29: an earlier read wrongly
  called the mains "water-poor"; a crew member flagged the seaside water point, confirmed on a map zoom.)*
- **Central hub:** droplets by Occident and the central hub area.
- **Far left:** droplet near Schwarzkopf / The Levi's Warehouse.
- **Bottom-right:** best-watered corner — 2+ droplets along the Auditori Rockdelux / Exit B path.

## Toilets 🚻 (WC)
- Mains cluster: ~3 WC — top (by Estrella Damm, near medical/merch/NiN), seaside-left (by the water),
  centre (by the AT cluster), plus one right by Revolut Balcony.
- Far left: WC by Port, WC by Schwarzkopf.
- Central: WC by Occident, WC by the 501/central VIP block.
- Bottom-left: WC by The Backstage / Levi's Plaza.
- Bottom-right: WC by Auditori Rockdelux.

## Bars 🍺
- Densest at the mains (clusters of beer/cocktail/food between & behind Estrella Damm and Revolut).
- Aperol Spritz branded bar on the seaside strip (Aperol Island of Joy zone).
- General beer/cocktail bars cluster at: the **mains**, **Cupra/Occident central hub**, **Schwarzkopf**, and **Plaza** (red-roof bar/food units).
- ⚠️ The icon at **The Levi's Warehouse is the "barres accessibles / accessible bar"** — a bar designated for **reduced-mobility patrons**, a SEPARATE legend symbol from the normal 🍺 BAR. It is NOT a general beer bar; don't send people there for a standard pint (verified against the official map, 2026-06-01). Nearest general bars to the Warehouse = the central hub or Schwarzkopf. There is a water point at the Schwarzkopf/Warehouse corner.

## Fever House (experiential space, NOT a lineup stage — added 2026-05-29 announcement)
A new multisensory space by **Fever** (the ticketing partner) + creative collective NAKED SPACE —
a "hidden" inflatable installation **at the heart of the venue** (central; not pinned to a stage slug
in the feed, so no exact walk times). NOT in the lineup feed / planner — it has no headline sets, just:
- suspended headphones to tune into **multiple stages at once** (centralised listening lounge),
- a big inflatable centrepiece + a screen live-streaming performances/artist content,
- **local underground DJ sets** — programmed **18:00–19:00 each day** (from social-media flyer, 2026-05-30;
  all real Barcelona-scene club DJs, NOT in the GraphQL feed so they won't appear in the planner / clash-detect):
  - Thu **Hello Sasy** — BCN-born, Berlin-based; high-energy ghetto tech / breakbeat / techno / acid / electro
  - Fri **Sofy Suars** — Bolivian-born, BCN-based; deconstructed club / global bass (funk, kuduro, reggaeton, guaracha)
  - Sat **Fillo Deportaberta** — co-founder/resident of BCN queer collective MUSA; fast, mental acid & industrial
- food by **Mesa Lobo** (the bespoke new-BCN spot from the gastro lineup).
Open all days, **access subject to capacity**. Useful as a central break / chill / escape-the-crowd spot.
Note the DJ slots (18–19h) overlap early-evening lineup sets (e.g. Sat 18:00 = TNP for one crew member).

## Quick planning notes
- Auditori-heavy runs (Cameron Winter, Lucrecia Dalt, TNP) sit by the best water + toilets (bottom-right).
- Central hub (Cupra/Occident) is the all-rounder for a water + toilet + drink loop between sets.
- The mains DO have water (seaside-NW corner), but it's a hike across the big cluster — grab it on the
  way in rather than mid-set.
